Notes for Students & Sponsors

For Sponsors:

  • Direct 1-on-1 sponsorship without any middleman administration.
  • Funds transferred directly to student bank account. EOTO handles zero money.
  • Proof of academic performance is periodically reviewed by EOTO volunteers.

For Students:

  • All cases undergo two-stage background verification of financial need and academic record.
  • We review cases periodically to make sure student is progressing in their studies.
  • Complete respect for student dignity and privacy.
