How EOTO Operates

100% Transparent, Volunteer-Driven College Tuition Assistance in Kerala

01

Case Intake & Verification

Students in need submit their college fee structure and family financial background. Our core volunteer team verifies details directly with college authorities.

02

Public Case Publication

Verified cases are assigned a unique Case ID (e.g. EOTO-2026-012) and published on our website and WhatsApp coordination channel.

03

Direct Sponsorship & Settlement

When a sponsor chooses a case, funds are paid directly to the college or student bank account. Payment receipts are posted to close the case.

EOTO Official Bylaw Summary

1. Mission & Purpose: EOTO is a non-profit volunteer initiative dedicated solely to funding higher education tuition fees for underprivileged students in Kerala.


2. Case Selection Criteria: Cases are vetted based on academic record, family financial background verification, and official college fee structure proof.


3. Financial Transparency: Neither EOTO nor its volunteers handle middleman funds for profit. Payments are made directly to the student's college account or documented bank account.


4. Confidentiality & Dignity: Public listings highlight course, location, and fee requirements without compromising student dignity.
